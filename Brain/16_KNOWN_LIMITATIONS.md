# 16 - Known Limitations

1. **Demonstration Jurisdiction Rules**:
   - The Star Map contains 22 demonstration rules across Pune and Nagpur. It is not an exhaustive legal directory of Indian administrative law.
2. **English Language Only**:
   - Day 1 AI text understanding handles English text. Vernacular Indian languages (Marathi, Hindi) are planned for Day 3.
3. **Fixed Threshold Calibration**:
   - The Escape Velocity Engine uses heuristic combined thresholds (`0.7 * sim + 0.3 * kw_overlap`) rather than calibrated probabilistic Bayesian priors.
4. **No Automated Filing**:
   - The system advises where to route; it does not automatically submit RTI requests to central or state RTI portals.
